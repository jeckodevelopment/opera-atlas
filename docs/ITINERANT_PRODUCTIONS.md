# Produzioni itineranti (soluzione C)

One existing organising theatre, one canonical work, one permanent production ID. Optional production project_name and performance_format; optional actual_location (venue, municipality, region, official source URL) per performance. Ordinary data and routes remain unchanged. The owner is used for theatre filters and theatre counts, the actual municipality/region for geography. No virtual theatres or festival identities.

Reviewed OperaCamion 2026: 15 general-public performances, 13 in Roma, 1 Bracciano, 1 Valmontone. The labelled 31 May preview is excluded. June and September share one staging, distinct from concert and ordinary stagings. Sources: https://www.operaroma.it/spettacoli/il-barbiere-di-siviglia-operacamion/ and https://www.operaroma.it/spettacoli/il-barbiere-di-siviglia-operacamion-2/. This initial source-bound registry is a reviewed historical import, not yet an automatic discovery adapter for future touring seasons. No precise coordinates are inferred. CI covers graph validation, provenance, replay safety, exclusion of previews and stable venue identities. Publication follows the existing cached catalog workflow and frontend/observatory deploys. Manual UI smoke tests after deployment: open production, select Bracciano/Valmontone dates, verify place and owner, search geographic cities, verify one staging and 15 performances across geography and owner filters.
