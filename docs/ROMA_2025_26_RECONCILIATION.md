# Rome 2025/26 retrospective recovery

The official genre index is a mixed-season feed; its first two pages cannot
certify coverage of the historic programme. The source adapter formerly
excluded 2025/26 pages even where the theatre supplied exact showtimes.

Sources: [official season](https://www.operaroma.it/news/doppio-sogno-la-stagione2025-26-dellopera-di-roma/),
[historic opera index](https://www.operaroma.it/genre/opera/page/3/),
[summer 2026](https://www.operaroma.it/news/il-teatro-dellopera-al-circo-massimo/).

The 12 individually identified production URLs are explicitly queued for
controlled acquisition. This does not certify publication or full coverage:
official venue, composer, weekday, clock and the ordinary authority/editorial
checks remain mandatory. Aida belongs at Circo Massimo, not the Costanzi.
The official oratorio is staged; the admission policy still applies.
La vita nuda is already published. The Henze double programme and OperaCamion
require separate work/date and physical-location disambiguation; do not
publish them under the concert heading or default theatre. The latter,
other remaining 2026/27 titles and any candidate blocked by authority data
must remain visible as audit residuals rather than being declared complete.

The existing dedicated Rome recovery workflow runs the seed, detail scan,
work research and catalogue publication; after it completes, verify the
individual productions and showtimes against the official programme.
